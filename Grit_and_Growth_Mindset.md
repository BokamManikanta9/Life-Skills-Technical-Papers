# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit - Paraphrase the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.

The video explains that success is not just about talent, but about not giving up when things become difficult.  
People who stay committed to their goals and keep trying, even after failing, are the ones who achieve great results.



## 2. Growth Mindset - Paraphrase the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

The video says that a growth mindset means believing you can improve through effort and learning.  
Instead of saying “I can’t do this,” you learn from mistakes and treat challenges as chances to grow.



## 3. What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

Internal Locus of Control means believing that your actions and choices shape your life.  
The key message is that you feel more motivated when you focus on what you can control instead of blaming luck or others.



## 4. Growth Mindset - What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a growth mindset?

- Believe that improvement is possible.  
- Take on challenges instead of avoiding them.  
- Learn from mistakes.  
- Keep putting in effort, even when it feels hard.  
- Accept feedback and use it to grow.



## 5. What are your ideas to take action and build a Growth Mindset?

- Take responsibility for your own learning.  
- Stay patient with difficult problems and keep trying to understand them.  
- Write and practice code that you truly understand.  
- Treat mistakes as part of the learning process.  
- Look for answers using available resources before asking for help.
