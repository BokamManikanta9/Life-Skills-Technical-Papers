# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1

**Paraphrase the Grit video in your own words.**

The video explains that success is not only about talent or intelligence, but also about having grit. Grit means staying committed to a goal and continuing to work even when things become difficult. It shows that passion, patience, and consistent effort can help us achieve long-term goals. I understood that giving up early can stop us from reaching our potential.

---

## Question 2

**Paraphrase the Introduction to Growth Mindset video in your own words.**

The video explains that our abilities are not fixed and that we can improve them through effort, practice, and learning. A growth mindset helps us see challenges and mistakes as opportunities to improve instead of reasons to give up. It also showed the difference between believing that abilities cannot change and believing that they can develop. I understood that the way I think about learning can affect how much I improve.

---

## Question 3

**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

Internal Locus of Control means believing that my actions, choices, and effort can influence the results in my life. Instead of blaming everything on luck or other people, I should focus on the things that I can control. The key point I understood from the video is to take responsibility for my actions and decisions. This helps me stay motivated because I can focus on what I can do to improve a situation.

---

## Question 4

**What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a growth mindset?**

- Believe that abilities can be developed.
- Accept challenges instead of avoiding them.
- Learn from mistakes and failures.
- Focus on effort and the learning process.
- Take feedback positively.
- Keep practicing and improving.
- Replace negative thoughts with a learning attitude.
- Be patient with progress.
- Learn from other people's success.
- Do not give up when something becomes difficult.

---

## Question 5

**What are your ideas to take action and build a Growth Mindset?**

I will treat difficult programming problems as opportunities to learn instead of thinking that I cannot solve them. When I make mistakes in my code, I will understand the reason for the mistake and try again instead of getting frustrated. I will practice consistently, ask questions, take feedback, and improve my skills step by step. I will also focus on progress rather than comparing my current skills with someone else's level.
