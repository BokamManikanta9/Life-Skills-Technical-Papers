# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. Steps / Strategies to Do Active Listening

Active listening means giving full attention to the speaker and truly understanding what they are saying.

- Give full attention by focusing only on the speaker  
- Do not interrupt and allow them to finish speaking  
- Show interest using eye contact, nodding, or smiling  
- Try to understand both words and feelings  
- Ask simple questions to clarify, not to judge  
- Respond calmly and respectfully  


## 2. What is Reflective Listening?

Reflective listening is about understanding the message clearly and confirming it with the speaker.

- Listen carefully without interrupting  
- Pay attention to words as well as emotions  
- Rephrase the message in your own words  
- Acknowledge how the speaker feels  
- Ask questions if something is unclear  
- Confirm that your understanding is correct  


## 3. Obstacles in the Listening Process

These are common problems that prevent effective listening.

- Getting distracted by phones or surroundings  
- Thinking about replying instead of listening  
- Interrupting or jumping to conclusions  
- Judging or assuming too early  
- Letting emotions block understanding  
- Ignoring tone, emotions, or body language  


## 4. How Can You Improve Listening?

Listening can be improved with conscious practice.

- Remove distractions and focus on the speaker  
- Maintain eye contact and positive body language  
- Listen to understand, not just to reply  
- Avoid interrupting the speaker  
- Ask simple questions for clarity  
- Practice active listening regularly  


## 5. When Do You Use Passive Communication?

Passive communication is used to avoid conflict or maintain peace.

- When the topic is not very important  
- To avoid arguments or conflicts  
- When the other person is very emotional  
- To maintain peace in the situation  
- When dealing with authority figures  
- When the issue does not affect you directly  


## 6. When Do You Use Aggressive Communication?

Aggressive communication usually happens due to strong emotions.

- When you are angry or frustrated  
- When you want to control the conversation  
- When reacting emotionally to disagreement  
- When feeling threatened or defensive  
- When focusing on winning an argument  
- When stress causes harsh communication  


## 7. When Do You Use Passive-Aggressive Communication?

Passive-aggressive communication expresses anger indirectly.

- When you are upset but avoid speaking directly  
- When you want to avoid open conflict  
- When you feel ignored or unheard  
- When you agree outwardly but disagree inside  
- When using sarcasm or indirect comments  
- When avoiding responsibility for confrontation  


## 8. How Can You Make Your Communication Assertive?

Assertive communication is clear, honest, and respectful.

- Speak clearly and confidently  
- Express thoughts and feelings honestly  
- Use “I” statements instead of blaming  
- Respect others’ opinions while speaking  
- Stay calm and control emotions  
- Be firm but polite in responses
