**From the given video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?**

The most interesting idea for me was that big changes don’t begin with big actions. 
They start with very small habits. When a habit feels tiny and easy, it’s easier to continue, and over time those small steps create real change.


# 2. How can you use B = MAP to make new habits easier? What are M, A and P?

B = MAP means a behavior happens when **Motivation, Ability, and Prompt** come together.

- **M (Motivation)** – You want to do it. 
- **A (Ability)** – It feels simple and easy. 
- **P (Prompt)** – Something reminds you to do it. 

To build a habit, make it very easy and connect it to a clear reminder.


# 3. Why is it important to "Shine" or Celebrate after completing a habit?

Celebrating after finishing a habit makes you feel happy and proud. 
That good feeling tells your brain to repeat the action, which helps the habit stick.

The book suggests making a habit very small and simple.

# 7. How to make a habit harder to do?

To break a bad habit, make it difficult to continue. 
Add small obstacles, like keeping distractions away, so it becomes less tempting.
