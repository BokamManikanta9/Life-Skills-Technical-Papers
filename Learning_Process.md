## Question 3

**What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

-   **Active (Focused) Mode:** This mode is used when we pay full attention to learning or solving a problem. It helps us understand new concepts, write code, and solve questions step by step.
-   **Diffused Mode:** This mode works when we are relaxed, such as while walking, resting, or sleeping. It helps the brain connect ideas, improve creativity, and find solutions that we may not notice during focused study.

Both modes are important because focused mode helps us learn, while diffused mode helps us understand the bigger picture.

## Question 4

**According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?**

1.  Decide exactly what you want to learn.
2.  Break the skill into small and manageable parts.
3.  Learn only enough to correct your mistakes.
4.  Remove distractions and focus on practice.
5.  Practice the skill for at least 20 hours.
6.  Keep improving through regular practice and feedback.

-   Keep my phone on silent and reduce social media usage while studying.
