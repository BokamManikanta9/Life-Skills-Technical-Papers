## 1. Activities that help me relax (Calm Quadrant)

- I go for a short walk and listen to music to clear my mind.  
- I take proper sleep to feel refreshed and recharged.  
- I spend quality time with my parents and friends to feel peaceful.  
- I watch movies or scroll social media for light relaxation.  


## 2. Situations where I enter the Stress Quadrant

- During deadlines and last-minute submissions.  
- While learning something new that challenges me.  
- When I have multiple tasks to handle at once.  
- During exams or performance pressure.  


## 3. How I know I am in the Excitement Quadrant

- I feel energetic and motivated to work.  
- I stay fully focused and enjoy what I am doing.  
- I lose track of time while working.  
- I feel confident and happy during the activity.  


## 4. Sleep is My Superpower – Key Takeaways

- Sleep strengthens memory and learning.  
- Proper sleep improves focus and productivity.  
- Good sleep supports emotional balance.  
- Sleep helps the body recover and repair.  


## 5. Ideas I Can Implement to Sleep Better

- Follow a fixed sleep and wake-up schedule.  
- Avoid screens before going to bed.  
- Limit caffeine intake in the evening.  
- Keep my room calm, dark, and comfortable.  
- Practice light stretching or breathing before sleep.  


## 6. Brain-Changing Benefits of Exercise – Key Points

- Exercise improves brain function.  
- It strengthens memory and learning ability.  
- Physical activity reduces stress and anxiety.  
- Exercise boosts mood through positive chemicals.  
- It improves focus and concentration.  
- Regular exercise protects long-term brain health.  


## 7. Steps I Can Take to Exercise More

- Fix a specific time daily for exercise.  
- Start small and gradually increase intensity.  
- Choose simple activities like walking, jogging, or sports.  
- Reduce long sitting hours and move more often.  
- Exercise with friends to stay motivated and consistent.
