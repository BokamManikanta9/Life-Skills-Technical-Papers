## 1. Which point(s) were new to you?

- **Over-communication** – I learned that it is important to keep the team informed about any delays, issues, or requirement changes as soon as possible.
- **Deep work** – The idea of working with full focus without distractions was new to me and I realized it can help improve productivity and code quality.
- **Asking clear questions** – I understood that when asking for help, it is better to clearly explain the problem and also mention the solutions already tried.
- **Documenting requirements** – Writing down requirements and sharing them with the team is useful for clarity and future reference.
- **Being mindful of teammates** – Respecting teammates time and sending well-organized messages instead of many small ones helps improve communication.



## 2. Which area do you think you need to improve on? What are your ideas to make progress in that area?

- **Communication with the team** – I want to improve how I explain problems and updates. To do this, I will practice giving clearer explanations and include proper details when asking questions.
- **Asking better questions** – Sometimes I ask questions without providing enough context. I will try to describe the issue clearly and mention the solutions I already tried before asking for help.
- **Maintaining focus while working** – I sometimes get distracted during work. I will keep my phone on silent and avoid social media during working hours to stay focused.
- **Documenting requirements** – I need to get better at taking notes during discussions. I plan to maintain proper notes and share them with my team so everyone has a clear reference.
